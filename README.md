# Autumn Preserves

*Put autumn in a jar.*

A cozy pixel-art game about **Granny Tonty**, who lives in **Everfall**, a small valley where autumn never ends. Outside the valley it is winter, so she fills jars with the endless autumn and sends them to everyone who needs them: her grandchildren in the city and her neighbours in town.

Made for the **KODTÜ 7** game jam (16–18 October 2026). Work in progress.

## How it plays

- **Explore and forage.** Every morning the forest is generated anew from a seed. Shake fruit trees, pick blackberries, find mushroom rings and rare varieties.
- **Cook and preserve.** Turn what you gather into jams, chutneys, pickles and brines through small kitchen mini-games. Every recipe, ingredient variety and add-in combination becomes a procedurally generated preserve with its own name, jar, taste profile and value.
- **Keep a tidy pantry.** Arrange your jars on the shelves like a little puzzle. The right shelf keeps food fresh, and a beautiful pantry gives Granny more energy the next morning.
- **Share it.** Fill your neighbours' orders by taste ("something sweet and spicy"), sell at the grocer and fill your recipe book.

No death, no penalties, no clock. Days end when you go to bed.

## Jam constraints

| Constraint | In the game |
|---|---|
| Pixelated art style | Everything is pixel art at 480×270 with integer scaling |
| Autumn colour palette | One 31-colour palette: [Vintage Autumn](https://lospec.com/palette-list/vintage-autumn) by Goopy25 |
| Procedural generation | Daily forest maps, per-save ingredient varieties, generated preserves (name, jar art, taste, value), orders and weather. The seed is always visible and can be explored in the **Seed Lab** |

## Run it

1. Install [Godot 4.7](https://godotengine.org/).
2. Open `game/project.godot` in the editor, or run `godot --path game`.
